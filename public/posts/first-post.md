# Introduction

Hi, my name is Marcelo. Welcome to my personal website.

I originally envisioned this website solely as a portfolio for future employers. Over time, I realized I wanted something different: a place to document my projects, share ideas, and archive my thoughts. 

Since this is my first post, I thought I'd share where I'm at in life. I decided to step away from a role where I was working as a software engineer at Boeing. I've learned quite a lot about myself; however, I felt it was time to move on as I wasn't applying all the skills that I wanted to apply at work. I was tasked with different activities that ranged from software testing, flight software development, and DevSecOps. Although, these are very interesting fields in their own right, these are areas I don't want to specialize in. Although, I'm grateful for the opportunity to work on very interesting projects, it was really time for me to start pursuing a role where I apply my love for mechanical engineering

Many people will wonder why leave software engineering, when the compensation is higher than in mechanical engineering. I think they see the software engineering salaries from California (where the cost of living is very high) and assume everyone is making that much money elsewhere. The reality is that the salaries in mechanical engineering compared to the salaries in software engineering are comparable; although it is true, software engineers make slightly more on average than mechanical engineers--it's not an enough motivator for me to stay in the field. Furthermore, I'll be much happier in a position where I'm doing what I love, which is working directly with physical systems.

As for my next steps, I'll be taking the FE exam which will enable me to brush up on my mechanical engineering knowledge. Since I'm preparing for the FE exam, I'm reading physics books related to thermodynamics and classical mechanics; I'm working through problems there and I'll be posting the solutions and notes here. And just like this post, I will often blog about things that are on my mind.

Marcelo
